# RealSailAway

RealSailAway is the more realistic version of my game, *Sail Away*. It is a Viking sailing game where you travel between islands, collect resources, upgrade your ship, and try not to get attacked by the Kraken. Follow along with the development at Macondo Hack Club! :)

## Story

A long time ago, Viking sailors traveled between islands to collect important supplies for their village. One island had wood, one had iron, and one had cloth and other useful resources.

Then a huge storm came, and sailors started disappearing in the ocean. People believed that the Kraken had woken up in the deep water.

Now, you are a Viking sailor who has been chosen to sail the old routes again.

You start with a small ship and have to travel to islands, collect resources, earn coins, and upgrade your ship. The farther you sail, the more dangerous the ocean gets. Watch out, because if you get too close to the Kraken, it can steal your resources!

## Features

This was a super fun project to make! I added a lot of new features that I think make the game much better.

* Sail NEW Viking ships around the ocean
* MASSIVE Island updates
* Collect resources like wood, iron, cloth, and coins through trading card packs
* Upgrade your ship so you can keep exploring
* Imoroved Save System
* Added the Kraken, which steals your resources if you get too close
* New effects on the islands
* Particle weather effects on the second island
* Wind effects on the third island

My favorite part of the game is probably the shop. I added a booster pack because I love trading card games, and the booster packs have hand-drawn cards in them!

I also really like the weather effects. The second island has particle weather, the first island has fire, and the third island has a wind effect.

## Controls

`W` Move forward
`S` Move backward
`A` Turn left
`D` Turn right
`H` Use the ship horn

## How to Play

1. Start with your ship.
2. Sail to one of the islands.
3. Dock and collect resources.
4. Earn coins.
5. Go to the shop.
6. Upgrade your ship or open a booster pack.
7. Sail farther, but be careful of the Kraken.
